Neon

Free: Free: 100 CU-hours per project, 1 GB storage per project (20 GB account total), scale-to-zero after 5 minutes (cannot be disabled).

  • Usage-based: Launch $0.106/CU-hour, Scale $0.222/CU-hour; storage $0.35/GB-month. 1 CU is about 4 GB RAM.
  • Rows here assume compute always on for 730 hours a month. With scale-to-zero, idle hours cost $0, so intermittent workloads pay far less (Neon lists an intermittent small database at about $15/mo on Launch).
  • Launch autoscales up to 16 CU (64 GB RAM).

Render Postgres

Free: Free: 256 MB RAM Postgres instance.

  • Instance price plus storage at $0.30/GB per month (storage is not bundled with the instance).
  • Workspace plan is extra: Hobby $0, Pro $25/mo. Bills here assume Hobby.
  • Larger instances up to 64 CPU / 384 GB are listed; rows here stop at 64 GB RAM.
