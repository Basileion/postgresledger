Verdict by workload

  • Hobby: 1 GB RAM, 10 GB storage: DigitalOcean is cheaper, $15.15 (1 GiB / 1 vCPU) vs $50.
  • Small production: 4 GB RAM, 50 GB storage: Heroku is cheaper, $50 (Standard 0) vs $60.90.
  • Medium: 8 GB RAM, 100 GB storage: DigitalOcean is cheaper, $122.10 (8 GiB / 4 vCPU) vs $200.
  • Large: 16 GB RAM, 250 GB storage: DigitalOcean is cheaper, $244.35 (16 GiB / 6 vCPU) vs $750.
  • XL: 32 GB RAM, 500 GB storage: only Heroku publishes a plan that fits.
  • High availability: 4 GB RAM, 50 GB storage: only Heroku publishes a plan that fits.

DigitalOcean Managed PostgreSQL

Free: No free tier for Managed Databases.

  • PostgreSQL Standard single-node prices, $/mo as listed. Extra storage $0.215/GiB/mo in 10 GiB steps, up to the top of each size's storage range.
  • Standby (HA) nodes are sold as additional nodes; their price was not listed as text, so HA is not modeled here.
  • Only the 5 sizes up to 16 GiB were listed on the page.

Heroku Postgres

Free: No free plan. Essential 0 is $5/mo (shared, 1 GB storage).

  • Classic plans bundle a storage cap; there is no per-GB add-on, so a bigger disk means a bigger plan.
  • Essential 0/1/2 ($5/$9/$20, 1/10/32 GB) run on shared resources with no stated RAM, so they are not in RAM-based comparisons.
  • Premium plans add high availability. Advanced tier pricing was not printed as text.
