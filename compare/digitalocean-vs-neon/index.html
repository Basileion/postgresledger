Verdict by workload

  • Hobby: 1 GB RAM, 10 GB storage: DigitalOcean is cheaper, $15.15 (1 GiB / 1 vCPU) vs $22.84.
  • Small production: 4 GB RAM, 50 GB storage: DigitalOcean is cheaper, $60.90 (4 GiB / 2 vCPU) vs $94.88.
  • Medium: 8 GB RAM, 100 GB storage: DigitalOcean is cheaper, $122.10 (8 GiB / 4 vCPU) vs $189.76.
  • Large: 16 GB RAM, 250 GB storage: DigitalOcean is cheaper, $244.35 (16 GiB / 6 vCPU) vs $397.02.
  • XL: 32 GB RAM, 500 GB storage: only Neon publishes a plan that fits.

DigitalOcean Managed PostgreSQL

Free: No free tier for Managed Databases.

  • PostgreSQL Standard single-node prices, $/mo as listed. Extra storage $0.215/GiB/mo in 10 GiB steps, up to the top of each size's storage range.
  • Standby (HA) nodes are sold as additional nodes; their price was not listed as text, so HA is not modeled here.
  • Only the 5 sizes up to 16 GiB were listed on the page.

Neon

Free: Free: 100 CU-hours per project, 1 GB storage per project (20 GB account total), scale-to-zero after 5 minutes (cannot be disabled).

  • Usage-based: Launch $0.106/CU-hour, Scale $0.222/CU-hour; storage $0.35/GB-month. 1 CU is about 4 GB RAM.
  • Rows here assume compute always on for 730 hours a month. With scale-to-zero, idle hours cost $0, so intermittent workloads pay far less (Neon lists an intermittent small database at about $15/mo on Launch).
  • Launch autoscales up to 16 CU (64 GB RAM).
