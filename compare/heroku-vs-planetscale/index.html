Verdict by workload

PlanetScale figures are compute only; its extra storage rate is not printed, so verdicts involving it understate its bill.

  • Hobby: 1 GB RAM, 10 GB storage: PlanetScale is cheaper, $10 (PS-10 arm64 single node) vs $50.
  • Small production: 4 GB RAM, 50 GB storage: PlanetScale is cheaper, $28 (PS-40 arm64 single node) vs $50.
  • Medium: 8 GB RAM, 100 GB storage: PlanetScale is cheaper, $50 (PS-80 arm64 single node) vs $200.

Heroku Postgres

Free: No free plan. Essential 0 is $5/mo (shared, 1 GB storage).

  • Classic plans bundle a storage cap; there is no per-GB add-on, so a bigger disk means a bigger plan.
  • Essential 0/1/2 ($5/$9/$20, 1/10/32 GB) run on shared resources with no stated RAM, so they are not in RAM-based comparisons.
  • Premium plans add high availability. Advanced tier pricing was not printed as text.

PlanetScale Postgres

Free: No free tier listed for Postgres.

  • AWS us-east-1 prices, arm64 (x86-64 costs the same or more). HA = 1 primary + 2 replicas.
  • The SKU price includes a starting network volume but not extra EBS storage, backups or egress; the extra-storage rate is not printed on the page, so bills here are compute only and understate storage-heavy setups.

† PlanetScale bills are compute only (extra storage rate not printed), so it is shown but not ranked. Aiven and DigitalOcean rows cover only the plans printed as text; see each provider page. Neon rows assume compute always on (730 h/mo). Prices are list, monthly, USD, before tax and egress.
