Heroku Postgres

Free: No free plan. Essential 0 is $5/mo (shared, 1 GB storage).

  • Classic plans bundle a storage cap; there is no per-GB add-on, so a bigger disk means a bigger plan.
  • Essential 0/1/2 ($5/$9/$20, 1/10/32 GB) run on shared resources with no stated RAM, so they are not in RAM-based comparisons.
  • Premium plans add high availability. Advanced tier pricing was not printed as text.

Supabase

Free: Free: 500 MB database, shared CPU, 500 MB RAM; paused after 1 week of inactivity; 2 active projects.

  • Pro plan $25/mo includes $10/mo compute credit (covers one Micro) and 8 GB disk per project, then $0.125/GB.
  • Bill here = $25 plan + compute add-on - $10 credit + extra disk, for one project.
  • Point-in-time recovery is $100/mo per 7 days of retention (not included).
