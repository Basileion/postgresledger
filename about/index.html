Methodology

  • Prices come from each provider's public pricing page and are stored with the source URL and check date (2026-11-20).
  • For a workload (minimum RAM, storage, optional HA), each plan with enough RAM is priced as its monthly price plus any published per-GB storage beyond what it includes. Plans that cannot reach the storage are skipped. The cheapest plan per provider is shown.
  • Usage-billed compute (Neon) is priced always-on at 730 hours a month; scale-to-zero lowers real bills for idle databases.
  • Where part of a bill is not printed as text (PlanetScale extra storage, larger Aiven plans, DigitalOcean standby nodes) the row is marked † and the limitation is stated on the provider page.
  • Monthly list price, USD, before tax, backups beyond defaults, and egress. No estimated or invented numbers.
